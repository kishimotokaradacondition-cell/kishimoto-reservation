# 柔整療養費 令和8年改定「8ヶ月9部位」（償還払い変更基準）: how industry sources and レセコン vendors explain it

Research date: 2026-10-30. Method note: the session's network blocked direct fetches of every target site (itsuki-s.jp, jba-kyoukai.or.jp, hone-u.com, zenjukyo.gr.jp, bondsmedical.net, izking.com, jusei-news.com, mhlw.go.jp all failed DNS). **All findings below come from web-search result snippets and summaries, not from full-page reads.** The search summaries were sometimes inconsistent with each other (flagged below). ## Q1. Does the rule exist, and how do industry sources describe the counting and reset?

### Takeaway
The rule exists. It is not a fee-reduction rule effective in July 2026. It is a new **criterion for switching a patient from 受領委任 to 償還払い**. It was set in the 令和8年 revision (notices of 令和8年6月1日; the revision took effect 7月1日), but **the criterion applies from 令和9年(2027)年1月**. The wording is: 「令和9年1月以降において、**前月までの連続する12か月の間に、通算して8月以上かつ（累積で合計）9部位以上**について施術を受けている患者」. The window is a **rolling 12 months ending with the previous month**. It is not cumulative from January 2026. So the count cannot keep growing past 12 months, and old months drop off as the window slides. The "January 2026 start" heard in the industry follows from this: the first judgment month is January 2027, and its window is January to December 2026.

### Cited Findings
- 2026 revision: notices amended 令和8年6月1日 and effective 令和8年7月1日. They add a 償還払い candidate category: 「前月までの連続する12か月の間に、通算8か月以上かつ通算9部位以上」. It applies 「令和９年１月以降」 — [World-sys（れ・セボーン）](https://www.world-sys.co.jp/topics/news_2200/); [日本柔整鍼灸協会 2517](https://jba-kyoukai.or.jp/blog/2517/); [itsuki-s](https://www.itsuki-s.jp/blog/p-2991/); [鹿大前整骨院](https://miyahara-jusei.com/%E6%95%B4%E9%AA%A8%E9%99%A2%E3%81%A7%E5%84%9F%E9%82%84%E6%89%95%E3%81%84%E3%81%AB%E5%A4%89%E6%9B%B4%E3%81%95%E3%82%8C%E3%82%8B8%E3%83%B6%E6%9C%889%E9%83%A8%E4%BD%8D%E3%81%AE%E5%9F%BA%E6%BA%96%E3%81%A8/)
- JBA's wording: 「令和9年1月以降において、前月までの連続する12か月の間に、通算して8月以上かつ累積で合計9部位以上について施術を受けている患者」 — [日本柔整鍼灸協会 2533](https://jba-kyoukai.or.jp/blog/2533/)
- The window is the 12 months back from the judgment month, not a calendar year. For a 令和9年1月 judgment the window is 令和8年1月～12月, and it shifts one month at a time after that — [日本柔整鍼灸協会 2533](https://jba-kyoukai.or.jp/blog/2533/) (as summarized by search). One search summary instead attributed a "March 2026 to February 2027" example to JBA. That contradicts both the 「前月まで」 wording and MHLW 問13, so it is probably a summarization error.
- MHLW 事務連絡 「柔道整復施術療養費に係る疑義解釈資料の送付について」 (令和8年7月1日) has a 問13 on how to calculate 「通算して8月以上かつ9部位以上」. Its example: for a 令和9年1月 judgment the window is 令和8年1月～令和8年12月. Per the snippet, the answer also covers a part that healed (治癒) and was later newly injured again. The exact clause was cut off — [MHLW 事務連絡 PDF](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/bunya/iryouhoken/iryouhoken13/dl/260702_01.pdf)
- "Because the judgment uses the most recent 12 months, counting effectively starts from 令和8年1月" — [itsuki-s](https://www.itsuki-s.jp/blog/p-2991/) (via search summary)
- How 部位 are counted (industry reading): each newly occurring injured part counts as **1 part in its 初検 month**. A part that continues over several months is **not** counted again. Worked example: new parts by month 2 → 1 → 2 → 3 = 8, and one more new part reaches 9 — [日本柔整鍼灸協会 2533](https://jba-kyoukai.or.jp/blog/2533/)
- Another explainer: the count is the total of 「すべての新規負傷部位」 written on claims in the 12-month window. A re-injury after 治癒 counts as a new 1 part — [search result citing itsuki-s / JBA](https://www.itsuki-s.jp/blog/p-2991/)
- Wording conflict: some sources say 「累積新規負傷 9部位」 and others 「延べ9部位」. 延べ could be read as counting part×month. Search summaries flagged this inconsistency and recommended checking the source text — [jitsumu-guide](https://jitsumu-guide.com/treatment-fee-revision-2026-guide/); [sakura-saku.org](https://sakura-saku.org/information/%E6%9F%94%E9%81%93%E6%95%B4%E5%BE%A9%E7%99%82%E9%A4%8A%E8%B2%BB-%E4%BB%A4%E5%92%8C%EF%BC%98%E5%B9%B4%EF%BC%97%E6%9C%88%E6%96%99%E9%87%91%E3%83%BB%E5%88%B6%E5%BA%A6%E6%94%B9%E5%AE%9A%E3%81%AE%E3%81%8A/)
- Purpose: to counter 「部位転がし」, the practice of rotating or adding new injured parts to keep claiming — [社会保険研究所 note](https://media.shaho.co.jp/n/n83738b9a418c?gs=eb9be6d517075bda4d495a7857acef44); background: [健保連 幸野委員提出資料 R8.1.30「部位転がし」](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/12404000/001646872.pdf)
- "Reset": a note.com writer says skipping a month or changing parts does not escape it, because the data accumulates at the insurer side and is judged on the rolling 12 months — [note: ReKuu 整体師KAI](https://note.com/keen_robin4684/n/na129bdfda713). Separately, the "reset" in some articles (「5か月の内、10回未満の月がある場合、カウントがリセット」) belongs to the **different** 長期・頻回継続 逓減 rule. Do not confuse the two — [大黒 コラム](https://anzen.e-daikoku.com/news/8853/)
- Meeting the criterion does **not** mean an automatic switch. The flow is a 注意喚起 notice to the patient and clinic, then fact-checking, then an individual decision by the insurer. 審査会 also treat it as a focus item — [world-hoken](https://www.world-hoken.co.jp/topics/131/); [World-sys](https://www.world-sys.co.jp/topics/news_2200/)
- The older 償還払い criterion (for comparison): 「初検日を含む月以降5ヶ月を超えて、かつ、1月あたり10回以上の施術を継続」 — [東京都電機健保 R6.9.24](https://www.denkikenpo.or.jp/UploadedFiles/20240924_topic.pdf)

### Inferences
- Rolling window: the months count is capped at 12 by construction. A displayed "9ヶ月" means 9 of the last 12 (or fewer) months had treatment. It cannot honestly reach "15ヶ月" or "20ヶ月". If a レセコン shows a months value above 12, it is counting cumulatively from a fixed start (e.g., 2026-01), **not** by the official criterion. If the user's レセコン showed "9ヶ月10部位" in October 2026, it plausibly reflects January to September or October 2026, which equals the rolling window at that point. Whether it keeps growing past 12 depends on the vendor's logic, which needs checking.
- The parts count can only fall when old 初検 months drop out of the 12-month window. Under the "new part counted in 初検 month" reading, a part first examined in Feb 2026 stops counting from the Feb 2027 judgment (window Feb 2026 to Jan 2027 still includes it; it drops at the Mar 2027 judgment, window Mar 2026 to Feb 2027), even if treatment of that part continues. This is inference. Whether a part that continues inside the window but began before it counts as 1 part is **not confirmed** (see Gaps).
- Practical self-check that matches the rule: for judgment month M, take months M-12 to M-1. Count the months with any 柔整 施術 (≥8?) and the number of new 負傷部位 with 初検 in those months (≥9?). Both conditions must hold.
- Worked example (illustrative, my construction based on the cited rule): patient treated every month Jan to Oct 2026, with new parts 3 (Jan), 2 (Mar), 2 (Jun), 3 (Sep) = 10. October display "9ヶ月10部位" would be consistent with Jan to Sep. At the 2027-01 judgment (window 2026-01 to 12): if treated every month, that is 12 months and 10+ parts, which meets the criterion. At the 2027-04 judgment (window 2026-04 to 2027-03): the Jan and Mar parts (5) drop out. That leaves 5 + any new ones; months are counted only within Apr 2026 to Mar 2027.

### Gaps
- The full verbatim answer to MHLW 問13 (治癒→再負傷, parts that continue across the window boundary, whether 初検 month must fall inside the window, multiple clinics or insurers) could not be read because of fetch blocks: [MHLW PDF](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/bunya/iryouhoken/iryouhoken13/dl/260702_01.pdf).
- Whether "8月" counts any month with ≥1 施術 day, or a month with a claim submitted, is not explicitly confirmed in the snippets (most likely the claim or 施術 month).
- Whether the count is per insurer only (the insurer sees only its own claims), i.e., whether it resets on an insurance change, was not found. It is likely per insurer in practice, since the insurer runs the judgment, but this is unconfirmed.

## Q2. What do レセコン vendors say about displaying the new count?

### Takeaway
Little public vendor documentation was retrievable. Vendors were "preparing" functions as of mid-2026. One vendor (ボンズメディカル) published a 償還払い対象者抽出マニュアル, and one (Feel) mentioned confirming last-1-year visit months and total parts. No vendor page found says explicitly whether its display is cumulative from 2026-01 or a rolling window. The named vendors (三四六, Sun柔整/柔整王, レセONE, 柔整ネット, ジャストシステム系, Medi) yielded no specific results.

### Cited Findings
- World-sys 「れ・セボーン」: says aggregating 12 consecutive months of long-term or multi-part data affects レセコン operation, and that development and preparation were in progress as of 2026-06-10 — [World-sys](https://www.world-sys.co.jp/topics/news_2200/)
- ボンズメディカル: published a 償還払い対象者抽出 manual (title only seen via search) — [ボンズメディカル お知らせ](https://www.bondsmedical.net/%E3%81%8A%E7%9F%A5%E3%82%89%E3%81%9B)
- レセコン "Feel": per a search summary, it was preparing a way to check the number of visit months and the total parts count over the most recent year (直近1年間の来院月数や通算部位数). Source attribution in the summary was unclear; possibly [日本柔整鍼灸協会 2533](https://jba-kyoukai.or.jp/blog/2533/) or [compassnet Action Plus](https://compassnet.jp/)
- Since 2025, レセプト forms have a 「継続月数」 field. It belongs to the 長期・頻回 50% 逓減 rule and is unrelated to the 12-month/9-part count. Clinics may confuse the two displays — [大黒 コラム](https://anzen.e-daikoku.com/news/8853/)

### Inferences
- A display that keeps rising past 12 months would contradict the rolling rule. Possible causes: the vendor uses a cumulative counter from 2026-01 (a provisional implementation), or the field is a different counter such as 継続月数 for the 逓減 rule. The user should ask their vendor: "Is this the count for 判定月の前月までの直近12か月, and which month is the judgment month?"

### Gaps
- No release notes or FAQ were found for 三四六, Sun柔整/柔整王, レセONE, 柔整ネット, ジャストシステム系 or Medi. They are possibly behind member logins, and fetching was blocked anyway.

## Q4. Practical guidance for clinics

### Takeaway
Industry guidance amounts to tracking each patient over the **most recent 12 months**, not since January 2026. Count months with treatment (threshold 8) and new injured parts (threshold 9). Watch especially for patients approaching the thresholds ahead of 2027-01, the first judgment month.

### Cited Findings
- Judgment uses 「直近1年間で、8か月以上来院しており、合計9部位以上」 — [itsuki-s](https://www.itsuki-s.jp/blog/p-2991/)
- Insurer-side data accumulates, so gaps or part changes do not evade the rule — [note ReKuu](https://note.com/keen_robin4684/n/na129bdfda713)
- Being a candidate leads to a notice and a fact check. It does not cause an immediate switch — [world-hoken](https://www.world-hoken.co.jp/topics/131/)

### Inferences
- Simple clinic rule: each month, for every insured patient, look at the previous 12 months only. Count (a) months with 施術 and (b) new 負傷部位 whose 初検 fell in those months. Flag at 7 months or 8 parts as "approaching". If the レセコン shows months > 12, treat it as a cumulative counter and recompute manually on the rolling basis until the vendor confirms.

### Gaps
- No association (日整, 全柔協, 各県会) published worked tables that could be retrieved. Any such tables are likely in members-only materials.
